# Bella Nails Studio

The supplied brand board is the visual authority. The app uses its stacked Bella
wordmark, gold full stop, four-petal symbol and the phrase “Your next little ritual”.

Gold (#AA8B4C) is decorative: the logo dot, fine rules and seal arc. Forest ink
owns text and primary actions. Functional focus and borders use darker supporting
tones so pale palette colours do not carry essential controls by themselves.

Bodoni Moda carries the wordmark, headings and italic brand statements. Manrope
carries navigation, forms, prices and body text. Fonts are self-hosted with their
SIL Open Font Licences in `assets/fonts/`.

`salon-booking-app.css` owns shared colour tokens. `bella-brand.css` applies the
reference identity and local font faces across customer and owner screens.

Reusable assets:

- `assets/bella-symbol.svg`: beige north petal, blush east, forest south, olive west.
- `assets/bella-icon.svg`: symbol on the forest app-icon tile.
- `assets/bella-seal.svg`: circular studio name with a gold arc.
- `assets/bella-monogram.svg`: secondary b-and-gold-dot mark.
- `assets/bella-manicure.webp`: AI-generated editorial photography, not a real
  client or a representation of work performed by the salon. Optimised to 1200×800.

Use generous porcelain space, restrained gold details, soft cards and forest
pill-shaped primary buttons. Keep UK English, London time and the configured
GBP/EUR salon currency. Offline preview remains enabled until explicitly changed.
